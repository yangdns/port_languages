In BP, syllable-final m/n are not pronounced as separate consonants; they nasalize the preceding vowel.

附录：巴葡与欧葡主要发音差异 / Appendix: BP vs. EP differences

差异点巴葡 (BP)欧葡 (EP)
词尾 -e (非重读)读 /i/：cidade /si.ˈda.dʒi/读 /ɨ/：cidade /si.ˈða.ðɨ/
词尾 -o (非重读)读 /u/：tudo /ˈtu.du/读 /u/：tudo /ˈtu.ðu/
t + e/i腭化为 /tʃ/：gente /ˈʒẽ.tʃi/不腭化：gente /ˈʒẽ.tɨ/
d + e/i腭化为 /dʒ/：cidade /si.ˈda.dʒi/不腭化：cidade /si.ˈða.ðɨ/
-r (词末)发 /ʁ/ 或 /ɾ/一般发 /ɾ/
